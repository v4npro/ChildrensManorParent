Privacy Policy

Childrens Manor - Unofficial (Manor Parent) · Effective September 30, 2026

This app is an unofficial iPhone companion for the Children’s Manor parent portal. It is not published or endorsed by Children’s Manor Montessori.

We do not run a server for this app and we do not collect, sell, or share your data.

What this app stores on your iPhone

  • School username and passcode. After you sign in on the school’s page, the app can save those credentials in the iPhone Keychain so Face ID can fill them later. They stay on this device and are not sent to us.
  • Face ID / device passcode. Used only to unlock the app when you return to it, and to fill the saved school login. Authentication runs on the device through iOS.
  • Settings. Preferences such as Simpler Check In / Out are stored in iOS UserDefaults on this device.
  • Classroom code reminder. Optional text you type is stored on this device. It is not sent to the school or to us.
  • Portal session. The school website may keep its own login cookie in the app’s WebKit data, the same way Safari would.

The school portal

The app opens the official portal at portal.childrensmanor.com. Anything you do there (sign-in, check in/out, messages) is handled by the school’s website under the school’s own policies. We do not receive a copy of that activity.

What we do not collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporters, or other third-party tracking SDKs. We do not create an account for you on our side.

Third parties

  • Children’s Manor / the school portal — receives the information you submit on their site.
  • Apple — provides iOS, Face ID, and Keychain on your device.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this policy page. Visiting this page is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

We do not share app data with anyone because we do not receive it.

Retention and deletion

On-device data lasts until you remove it. Delete the app from your iPhone to remove the Keychain login, WebKit cookies, settings, and code reminder. You can also turn off Face ID for this app in iOS Settings.

There is no account of yours on our servers to delete. To change or close your school parent account, contact the school.

Children

The app is for parents and guardians who already have a school portal login. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
